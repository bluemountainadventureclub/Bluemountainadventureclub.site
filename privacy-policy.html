01 — Overview

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the NovaScan — QR Generator mobile application (package name: com.novascan.qr.generator), developed and published by BLUE MOUNTAIN ADVENTURE CLUB (SMC PRIVATE) LIMITED.

We are committed to being transparent about how our app works. The short version: NovaScan does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information whatsoever. This policy exists to formally confirm that commitment.

02 — Data Collection

We Collect No Data

NovaScan does not collect any personal or non-personal data from its users. Specifically, the following are never collected, stored, or transmitted:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, device ID)
  • QR code content or scan history
  • Usage statistics or in-app behaviour
  • Location data of any kind
  • Device information or IP addresses
  • Crash logs or diagnostics sent to any server
All QR code generation happens entirely on your device. No data ever leaves your phone. NovaScan has no backend server, no database, and no cloud infrastructure tied to user data.
03 — Third Parties

No Third-Party Services

NovaScan does not integrate any third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, crash-reporting SDKs, or social media trackers.

  • No Google Analytics or Firebase
  • No Facebook SDK or Meta Pixel
  • No advertising networks or ad identifiers
  • No third-party crash reporting tools

Your use of NovaScan is not shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party under any circumstances.

04 — Children

Children's Privacy

NovaScan does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13, or under any higher age threshold required by applicable local law.

Since we collect no data from any user, NovaScan is inherently safe for users of all ages. No parental consent mechanism is required as there is nothing to collect.
