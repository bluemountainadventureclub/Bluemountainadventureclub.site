📋 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how BLUE MOUNTAIN ADVENTURE CLUB (SMC PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Aurus Key application ("App") available on the Google Play Store. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data we do and do not collect.

The short version Aurus Key is a fully offline, on-device app. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data — ever. All passwords are generated and exist only on your device.

🚫 Information We Do Not Collect

Aurus Key does not collect any of the following:

Personal identifiable information
Passwords you generate or save
Device identifiers or hardware IDs
Location data (GPS or IP-based)
Usage statistics or analytics
Crash reports or diagnostics
Advertising IDs (GAID)
Contacts or clipboard contents
Camera or microphone data
Network activity or browsing data

🔒 App Permissions

Aurus Key requests no Android permissions. The App does not access the internet, your contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or any other device resource. All password generation is performed entirely within the app's sandboxed environment using standard Android APIs.

  • No INTERNET permission — the app is fully offline
  • No READ/WRITE storage permissions
  • No location permissions
  • No camera or microphone permissions
  • No contacts or calendar access

⚙️ How the App Works

Aurus Key is a standalone, on-device tool that generates secure passwords based solely on the settings you choose within the app. The following technologies are used internally and do not involve any data transmission:

  • AndroidX Navigation Component — in-app screen navigation only
  • Material Design Components — user interface rendering only
  • ConstraintLayout and AppCompat — layout and compatibility only
  • SharedPreferences (if used) — stored locally on your device, never transmitted

No third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or remote configuration are integrated into this application.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Aurus Key does not integrate any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any analytics platform
  • No AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or any advertising SDK
  • No Crashlytics, Sentry, or any crash/error reporting service
  • No cloud storage, sync, or remote database services
  • No social media login or sharing SDKs

The only external interaction is the standard Google Play Store distribution channel, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

🛡️ Data Security

Because Aurus Key collects no data and performs no network communication, there is no user data at risk of interception, breach, or unauthorized access on our end. Any passwords generated or saved within the app reside exclusively on your personal device and are subject to your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

We strongly encourage users to keep their device software up to date and to use device-level encryption for maximum protection of locally stored data.

👶 Children's Privacy

Aurus Key is not directed at children under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect any information from children. Since the App collects no personal information from any user of any age, it inherently complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.
